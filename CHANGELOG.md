## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-23

### Added

- First release.
- Immutable matrices of real numbers: arithmetic, `transpose()`, `trace()`, `determinant()`, `inverse()` and `isSingular()`, and 4x4 transformations (`translation()`, `scaling()`, `rotationX()`, `rotationY()`, `rotationZ()`, `rotation()`) with `transform()` for `phpmath/vector`.
- A documentation site at https://phpmath.github.io/matrix/.
